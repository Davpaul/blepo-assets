# Chapmans end card rules (learned from Bossman's own edits, 1080x1920)

## Circle elements
1. Three elements at most per design: one orange or colour solid circle, one navy ring texture, one small fingerprint.
2. Never magenta or purple near the logo. Never a line-based pattern in the top section.
3. Solid circle: very large (about 700px, 0.64 of canvas width), pushed off the top right so only its lower curve shows. Its lowest point lands at the top of the logo (about y 350).
4. Navy rings: darkest navy #0C1F45, huge (about 1300px, 1.2x the canvas width), centred on the bottom edge and left of centre (centre about x 200). Only the top arc shows, rising to about y 1270.
5. Fingerprint: small (about 390px, 0.36 of canvas width), always 50% opacity, magenta, sitting behind the "In partnership with" box in the right half (centre about x 880, y 1240). Not in a corner.
6. No two elements share a colour or pattern. Sizes run huge, large, small.
