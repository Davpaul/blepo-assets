# Chapmans circle element rules

1. Three circle elements at most on any one design.
2. Never put a magenta or purple element near the Chapmans logo (the logo is magenta). Near the logo use orange, white or navy tones.
3. Line-based patterns (fingerprint, rings) never sit in the top section near the logo. The top section takes a solid circle.
4. The fingerprint is always at 50% opacity.
5. No two elements share a colour or a pattern. One element is the darkest navy (#0C1F45, the "In partnership with" box colour), used as texture.
6. One large, one medium, one small. Let them run off the edges.
7. Keep text and pills clear. The logo runs large, about 800px wide on a 1080 canvas.
